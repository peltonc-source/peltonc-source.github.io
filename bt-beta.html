Bound Together — Android Beta

Bound Together is a free book club app: private clubs with an invite code, everyone's reading progress at a glance, spoiler-safe chat by book quarters, a discussion board, and voting on the next book. No ads, no feed.

It's live on iPhone now. The Android version is in closed beta testing — and that's where you come in. Google requires at least 12 testers to stay opted in for 14 days before the app can launch publicly on Android. Every tester genuinely moves the launch closer.

1Join the tester group

One tap, no approval needed. Use the same Google account for every step. This is what unlocks the app for your account.

2Become a tester & install

Tap "Become a tester", then install the app. If it says "App not available", wait a few minutes after Step 1 — Google takes a moment to catch up — then try again.

3Try it out

Create your own book club, or explore the app and tap around. Real use — even a few minutes here and there — is exactly what this test is for.

4Stay opted in for 14 days

This is the important one: please keep the app installed and stay opted in for the full 14 days. Opting out early restarts that tester's clock, and the launch needs 12 testers opted in at the same time.
