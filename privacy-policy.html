Privacy Policy — Real Social

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Real Social ("the App") is published by Casey Pelton. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: your display name. We use anonymous authentication — no email or password is required.
  • Group content: group names, member lists, chat messages, events, hangout logs, memories, life events, and birthday entries you create or contribute to. This content is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) and synced between members of your groups.
  • Device push token: an Expo push token so we can send you notifications about group activity. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Contacts (optional): if you grant permission, the App reads your contacts so you can quickly add friends to groups. Contact data is used only for this purpose and is never uploaded except for the specific people you choose to add.
  • Calendar (optional): if you grant permission, the App can add group events to your device calendar. We do not read your existing calendar entries.

How We Use Data

  • To create your account and sync your groups across your devices.
  • To send push notifications about messages, nudges, new group invites, and birthdays.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train machine-learning models.

Data Sharing

Your group content is visible to the other members of each group — that is the purpose of the App. We share data with our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase for storage/auth, Expo for push delivery) as needed to operate the service. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account exists. To delete your account and data, contact pelton.casey@gmail.com and we will remove your user document and group memberships within 30 days. Content you contributed to shared groups (e.g. messages) may remain visible to other members.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our providers. Firestore security rules restrict each group's data to its members only.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
