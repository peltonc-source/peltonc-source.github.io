Privacy Policy — Book Blast

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Book Blast ("the Game") is published by Casey Pelton. This policy explains what data the Game collects and how it is used. The short version: it collects nothing.

Data We Collect

None. Book Blast has no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no ads. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifier, or any other personal information, and we do not send any data from your device to us or to any third party.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your game progress — Dewey and your other owls, your Bookmarks, your book collection, and the furniture in Dewey's nook — is saved locally on your device so it is still there when you reopen the Game. That information never leaves your device.

Purchases

Book Blast is free to download with no ads. The optional Library Patron unlock is a one-time $2.99 purchase, handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment information from that purchase. Bookmarks are pretend in-game currency with no cash value.

Book Titles

Book titles and author names appear as plain text on the book spines. No cover art is used, and the rights to the books remain with their authors and publishers.

Children

Because the Game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the Game. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
