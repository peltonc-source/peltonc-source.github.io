Bound Together — Support

Bound Together is the app for reading books together. Enjoy a book together.

Joining a book club

Ask a club member for the invite code (6 characters) and enter it on the app's join screen. If someone sends you an invite link, just tap it — with the app installed it opens straight to a one-tap join confirmation. Without the app, the link shows the code and store buttons instead.

Tracking your progress

Open your club's current book and drag the progress bar (or enter a page number) to update where you are. Your progress syncs to the rest of the club so everyone stays on the same page.

Nominating and voting on books

Nominate a book by typing its title and tapping the matching result — the title, author, and cover fill in automatically. When voting opens, pick your favorites and the club reads the winner next.

Not getting notifications?

Check your device settings: Settings → Notifications → Bound Together, and make sure notifications are allowed. New books, votes, and club activity arrive as push notifications.

Buying books in the app

Buying through the in-app Amazon links keeps the app free — we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Deleting your data

Email pelton.casey@gmail.com and we will remove your user document and club memberships within 30 days. Content you contributed to shared clubs (e.g. messages) may remain visible to other members. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Something broken?

Email pelton.casey@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what happened — screenshots help.
