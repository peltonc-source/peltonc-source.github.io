Child Safety Standards — Real Social Circle

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Real Social Circle is a private friendship app intended for adults (18+). We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form.

1. Prohibited content

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, and any sexual content involving minors are strictly prohibited.

2. Reporting

Any member can report another member's message or post from inside the app: long-press the message and tap Report. Reports are sent to the developer for review.

3. Blocking

Members can block any other member. Blocked users' content is hidden from the person who blocked them. Blocked users are managed in Settings → Blocked users.

4. Enforcement

Accounts found sharing CSAM or engaging in child exploitation are removed immediately and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

5. Private groups

All groups are private and invite-only. Only invited members can see a group's content.

6. Contact

To report a child safety concern, email pelton.casey@gmail.com.
