Real Social Circle — Support

Real Social Circle is the app that helps you stay in touch with friends and family — for real. No feeds, no likes, no algorithms.

Joining a group

Ask a group member for the invite code (6 characters) and enter it on the app's join screen. If someone sends you an invite link, just tap it — with the app installed it opens straight to a one-tap join confirmation.

Not getting notifications?

Check your device settings: Settings → Notifications → Real Social, and make sure notifications are allowed. Birthday reminders and group activity arrive as push notifications.

Buying gifts in the app

Buying through the in-app Amazon links keeps the app free — we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Deleting your data

Email pelton.casey@gmail.com and we will remove your user document and group memberships within 30 days. Content you contributed to shared groups (e.g. messages) may remain visible to other members. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Something broken?

Email pelton.casey@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and what happened — screenshots help.
