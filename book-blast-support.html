Support — Book Blast

Book Blast is a bookshelf block-puzzle game by Casey Pelton: drag stacks of real books onto the shelf, fill whole rows and columns to check them out, and raise Dewey, the library owl.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, and suggestions: pelton.casey@gmail.com

Common Questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Book Blast has no accounts and collects no data, so progress — your owls, Bookmarks, collection, and furniture — lives in the app on your phone and is not backed up to any server.

How do I start over?
Tap the "?" button in the app and choose the erase-save option at the bottom of the help panel. It asks twice before deleting anything.

Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Everything runs on your device.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No ads, and no in-app purchases. The game is free.

The sound is quiet or silent.
The game respects your phone's silent switch. Flip silent mode off, and check the Sound and Music toggles on the Play tab.
